title: Do Not Outsource Hiring To AI

I see more and more companies do automated interviews and challenges with AI. Often even unsupervised by humans. I think this is a big mistake: If you use a machine to hire humans, you hire machines.

Do not delegate your most important tasks to AI! Hiring is one of them.

As a manager/founder, three of the most important tasks are:
1. Hire a high-performing team of strong individuals who complement each other.
2. Make sure they actually perform and are getting better.
3. Fire the ones who are not able to perform on your team. They would probably perform better in another team, so don't hold them back.

I know hiring is super hard. I did 80 interviews and looked through 100s of profiles in order to hire for two roles on my current team. But it was worth it: One actual hire was not from my extended network. It was a cold application. An AI would probably have filtered them out. Hiring calls also give insight into the state of the industry. It's a form of networking: I kept in touch with some candidates and will definitely reach out to them, once a role opens.

Also, hiring is not something a machine is good at because it is more about a gut feeling than numbers. It's not about the highest grade at the best university or how many lines of code a candidate can write. Or how quickly they can create some slides. It's about vibes: Do they understand the cultural context, are they excited about our vision, are they inspiring, do they complement the existing team well, are you excited to work with them? None of these questions an AI can answer.

Of course, AI can help tremendously in the process: Source candidates, transcribe interviews, do follow-ups, drill down into a candidate's past work, etc. But as a hiring manager, you need to look at their profiles and go on a call with them.

Stay in the driving seat of the hiring process and let AI assist you, not take over. If you use a machine to hire humans, you hire machines. Keep hiring humans!
