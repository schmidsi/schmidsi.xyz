ENSv2 will be activated tomorrow and I'm proud to be part of the biggest upgrade of ENS in its history! It's not only the smart contracts that have been redesigned and rewritten to lay the foundation of the open naming layer for the internet: a flexible permissions system, a proper extensible tree structure and more. There are also two new apps, the manager and explorer, which will give the two distinct user groups, normies and power users, the interface they need. 

On top of that, and here is my personal contribution, we made sure that wallets and apps which integrate with ENS not only become ready for ENSv2 but also deepen their integration: Use the Universal Resolver, support DNS-imported names, do offchain resolution properly and many more. The ENS integrations ecosystem got an upgrade too 💥.

I'm also grateful to be working under the leadership of Nick Johnson (@nicksdjohnson), Jeff Lau (@Jefflau), Katherine Wu (@katherinewu) and Dom (@domicoeth) who had to take some tough and controversial decisions like ditching NameChain or postponing the launch until we are 100% sure that we will be ready.

If you are an app developer and you are not sure if your app is ENSv2 ready: Don't fret: ENSv2 names will continue to be resolvable through the documented V1 resolution paths. And being fully ENSv2 compatible is in 90% of the times simply updating to the latest version of your favorite web3 library. If your app has writing capabilities like registering name, setting a text record or managing subnames, more work is needed. Head over to https://docs.ens.domains/web/ensv2-readiness/ to find out more or reach out. We're happy to assist.
