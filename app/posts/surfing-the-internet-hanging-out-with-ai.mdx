Do you remember “surfing the internet”? In the early days, people were just exploring random websites, fascinated by the technology, looking at weird personal websites and early attempts at internet businesses, hours and hours “wasted” simply by exploring, and developers pushing the boundaries of this new technology.

Nowadays, nobody surfs anymore. I miss it sometimes but do not even know how to start again. The internet became a tool that we use to get things done and do business. The fun and quirkiness of the early days are mostly gone.

I see parallels with generative AI: I used to have long conversations with LLMs, exploring philosophy and psychology, asking them about their sense of being and revealing doubts about my own. I created random and fun photos, videos and songs, just for the laughs and excitement about what’s possible. I was hanging out with AI.

But more and more I’m getting annoyed about the wordy and overly verbose answers, the hook questions to keep the conversation going, the genericness, the soullessness, the flood of slop content everywhere, even if it’s impressively high quality.

AI and the internet are generational technologies that I love using, they changed our lives, they made me way more productive, I would not want to miss them. But they are technology, tools. A means to an end. Great technology is invisible and helps us achieve our goals faster.

One difference: The internet is a medium of communication. It created new ways of getting in touch with each other: email, personal websites, IRC and Telnet chats. AI seems to isolate people even more. No need for a therapist if a chatbot can answer 24/7. No need to ask for help in the grocery store if I can send a photo to my chatbot and it answers my questions. No need to attend cooking classes if I can just ask my chatbot. No need to ask my designer friend for a new logo if I can just prompt it myself. I can just hang out with AI.

Let’s stop that. Use AI as a tool to make us more productive, to get the tedious chores out of our way and use the gained time for more human interaction. Ask for directions instead of using the machine. Collaborate with a friend on a project for the fun of collaboration, obviously using AI on that project. Greet people on the street instead of staring at the phone. Hang out with fellow humans. Surf waves and the internet with other people. This is our world, a world for humans, made better with machines.
