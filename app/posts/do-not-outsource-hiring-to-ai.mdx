I see more and more companies who are using AI to do automated inteviews and challenges with AI. Often even unsupervised by humans. I think this is a big mistake: If you use a machine to hire humans, you hire machines.

Do not delegate your most important tasks to AI! Hiring is one of them.

I know it's super hard. I did 80 interviews and looked through 100s of profiles in order to hire for two roles on my current team. But it was worth it.

As I said, for managers/founders, three of the most important tasks are:
1. Hire a high performing team of strong individuals who complement each other
2. Make sure they actually do perform high and increase performance over time
3. Fire the ones who are not able to perform in your team. They would probably perform better in another team, so don't hold them back.

Also hiring is not something a machine is good at because it is more about a gut-feeling than numbers. It's not about the highest grade at the best university or how many lines of code a candidate can write. Or how quickly they can create a power point presentation. It's about vibes: Do they understand the cultural context, are they excited about our vision, are they inspiring, do they complement the existing team well, are you excited to work with them? None of these questions an AI can answer.

Of course, AI can help tremendously in the process: Source candidates, transcribe interviews, do follow-ups, drill down into a candidates past work, etc. But as a hiring manager, you need to look at their profiles and go on a call with them.

Stay in the driving seat of the hiring process and let AI assist you, not overtake you.
